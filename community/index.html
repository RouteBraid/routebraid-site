RouteBraid Community Rules

  • Effective date: August 20, 2026
  • Last updated: August 20, 2026
  • Safety contact: RouteBraid@gmail.com

These rules apply when you create, accept, or use a RouteBraid trip invitation or shared itinerary. They form part of the RouteBraid Terms of Use.

Invite intentionally

  • Invite only people with whom you have a legitimate reason to share a trip.
  • Verify the recipient and role before sharing an invitation code.
  • Do not repeatedly invite someone who declined, left, or asked not to be contacted.
  • Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent who owns or is joining a trip.

Respect privacy and consent

  • Do not share another person's home address, live or planned location, reservation code, passport information, contact details, or other sensitive information without a legitimate purpose and appropriate permission.
  • Do not use trips, invitations, maps, or itinerary information to track, monitor, stalk, intimidate, or control another person.
  • Do not copy or redistribute a collaborator's information outside the trip without permission.

Keep content lawful and travel-related

  • Do not post threats, harassment, hate, exploitation, sexual content involving minors, fraud, instructions for violent wrongdoing, or other unlawful material.
  • Do not infringe intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, or other rights.
  • Do not use RouteBraid to facilitate trafficking, abuse, evasion of lawful authorities, or conduct that creates a credible safety risk.

Protect RouteBraid and other users

  • Do not scrape profiles or trips, spam invitation codes, automate abusive actions, bypass roles or blocks, probe security, or interfere with RouteBraid or its providers.
  • Do not share a code publicly if you intend the trip to remain private. Revoke a code you believe has been exposed.
  • Do not make knowingly false reports or misuse safety controls to retaliate against another user.

Reports and enforcement

Use available in-app leave, remove, block, and report controls. You may also report a concern to RouteBraid@gmail.com with enough information to identify the affected trip or account. Do not email passwords, full payment details, passport numbers, or unnecessary sensitive information.

RouteBraid may investigate, preserve relevant records, limit invitations, remove or restrict shared content, suspend an account, or contact appropriate authorities when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce these rules or the Terms, or comply with law. Enforcement decisions can consider severity, context, repetition, and risk. You may ask for reconsideration by emailing the safety contact.

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. RouteBraid is not an emergency or real-time monitoring service.
